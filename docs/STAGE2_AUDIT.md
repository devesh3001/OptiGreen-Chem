# Stage-2 audit of the OptiGreen-Chem repository

Audited commit: `dd137df` (github.com/devesh3001/OptiGreen-Chem). Every item below was
reproduced by running the code; the fix is in branch `stage2-real-data`. Items 1-15 are
fixed in place; 16-17 are in the legacy synthetic pipeline and are superseded by the Stage-2 evaluation.

| # | Where | Problem found | Effect | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `data/synthetic/*` | All inputs generated by `generator.py`; no real dataset anywhere | No result could be called evidence | Real EIA data layer: `src/optigreen/data/eia.py`, `realcase.py`, `network.py` |
| 2 | `scripts/run_phase4.py` | Imports `optigreen.forecasting.metrics` and `optigreen.optimization.inventory_sim`, which do not exist | Script crashes on start | Broken imports removed |

## Review of the earlier "Review 2 evidence" package (DataCo)

Kept: its audit points (#4, #5, #8), the idea of a greedy baseline and of re-scoring plans
against reality, and its honest reporting style.

Not kept as the project dataset: DataCo is retail order data (clothing, sports goods,
electronics) with no production plants, terminals or chemical products; its physical network
(4 plants, 8 warehouses, coordinates, costs, emission factors) had to be invented. Its own
results show the limitation: the planning-week P50 was 877.6 items against 1,802 observed
(45 % coverage), the XGBoost risk model (AUC 0.7515) was indistinguishable from a
shipping-mode lookup (0.7504), and the GAT showed no benefit.
